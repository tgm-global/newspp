About the 5 Portion Meal Tray Sealer

The 5 Portion Meal Tray Sealer is made for multi-compartment thali trays. One sheet of film closes all five sections together, so every dish stays in its own space and the whole meal arrives neat.

Cloud kitchens, canteens and caterers use this kind of machine to pack hundreds of meals quickly and safely.

Where it is used: Cloud kitchens, office and factory canteens, school and college mess, tiffin services, event and wedding catering, and bulk meal orders.

How to choose: Send us a sample 5 compartment tray, or its size and compartment layout. We will confirm the right mould and machine.

Key features

  • Seals 5-compartment thali and meal trays
  • All compartments close in a single press
  • Mould made for your tray layout
  • Stainless steel body, easy to clean
  • Built for daily, high-volume packing
